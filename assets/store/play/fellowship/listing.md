# Baluard Fellowship Play store listing

App name: Baluard Fellowship

Short description: Supporter access for invited friends and testers of Baluard.

Full description:

Baluard Fellowship is a companion app for invited friends, family, and testers who help polish Baluard.

While Fellowship is installed, the Google Play version of Baluard unlocks Supporter features, including vault sync, sharing, and TOTP codes. Removing Fellowship returns Baluard to its usual subscription checks.

Fellowship has no accounts, activation codes, ads, tracking, network access, vault access, or in-app purchases. It shows a thank-you screen, and Baluard checks that the companion is genuine before unlocking these features.

You need an invitation and a separate installation of Baluard Password Manager. Please don't buy Fellowship as a standalone product. It doesn't store passwords or manage a vault.
